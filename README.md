# salondesrefuses2026
Photos from the 23 July 2026 Salon des Refusés event. Established by the National Trust’s S.H. Ervin Gallery in 1992, Salon des Refusés provides an alternative exhibition for works entered into the Archibald and Wynne Prizes. Visitors to the annual Sydney exhibition vote for the recipient of the Holding Redlich People’s Choice Award.
